I’m Emma, an architectural engineer who is currently pursuing a PhD at the Predict and KNoWS groups at IDLab Ghent. My work focuses on fusing semantics and machine learning to enhance building management. This research is supervised by Femke Ongenae and Sofie Van Hoecke. Previously, my research centered on compliance checking of building models. Beyond academia, you’ll often find me at a spinning class or immersed in my newest knitting project!
